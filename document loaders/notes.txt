AI Course Notes — Summary

Topic: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
Source reference: AI_Info.pdf (Intro to AI lecture notes)

1. Search
- Covers problem formulation, deterministic and fully observable environments.
- Tree search algorithms: BFS, uniform-cost search, DFS, iterative deepening.
- Informed search: A* search, admissible heuristics, memory-bounded A*.
- Discusses graph search and handling repeated states.

2. Probabilities
- Basics: random variables, joint/marginal/conditional distributions, Bayes' theorem.
- Distributions: Bernoulli, Binomial, Beta, Multinomial, Dirichlet, conjugate priors.
- Continuous distributions: Gaussian, Dirac, particle approximations.
- Decision theory: utilities, entropy, KL divergence.
- Monte Carlo methods: rejection sampling, importance sampling.

3. Bandits, MCTS & Games
- Multi-armed bandits and the exploration-exploitation tradeoff.
- Upper Confidence Bound (UCB1) algorithm.
- Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) and Upper Confidence Trees (UCT).
- Application of MCTS to POMDPs.
- Game playing algorithms.
